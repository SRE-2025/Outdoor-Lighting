The Best Trees to Plant in Central Texas This Fall

Fall — not spring — is the best time to plant trees in Central Texas. Here are the toughest, most rewarding choices for our climate.

Home/Guides/The Best Trees to Plant in Central Texas...

The best time to plant a tree in Central Texas isn't spring — it's fall. Cooler air and warm soil let roots establish through winter, so the tree is ready to thrive before its first brutal summer. Plant now and you're years ahead.

Great shade trees for our climate

  • Live Oak — the iconic, drought-tough evergreen shade tree, though give it room and mind oak-wilt timing on pruning.
  • Cedar Elm — a tough native that handles our clay and heat and gives real fall color.
  • Bur Oak — fast-establishing, big-canopy shade with dramatic leaves.
  • Chinquapin Oak — handsome, adaptable, and well-suited to alkaline soils.

Smaller and ornamental

  • Texas Redbud — spring color and a manageable size for smaller yards.
  • Mexican Plum — native, fragrant early bloom, great for wildlife.
  • Desert Willow — long bloom, extremely drought-tough.

Plant it right

Dig wide, not deep; keep the root flare at grade; water deeply but infrequently to push roots down; and mulch the root zone (not against the trunk). Those few things decide whether a tree merely survives or truly thrives.

We help you choose and place the right trees, and care for the ones you have. See tree care services or plan a fall planting.
