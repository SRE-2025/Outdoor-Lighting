Our story

Born and raised in Central Texas

Owner Gye Hutson grew up in Austin near Lake Travis — Leander High School, then Austin Community College, then Southwestern University in Georgetown. He's spent his whole life in Central Texas, and it shows in the work.

In 1998, Gye started the family business in South Austin. As the landscaping and turf side grew into serious design-build work, it earned its own name — Research Turf Management, launched in 2007, the same year the company earned its BBB accreditation. Today both sides of the business run out of one warehouse in Buda, with the same office team and the same crews.

That local knowledge is the whole point. Gye knows Central Texas soil, Central Texas heat, and Central Texas drought firsthand — so the landscapes we design are built to actually thrive here, not just look good on install day.

Our philosophy

We treat every home as if it were our own — and every customer, including their four-legged family members, like neighbors.

Family and pet safety is the throughline in everything we do. It's why customers stay with us for 13, 20, even 30 years — and why they refer their neighbors.
