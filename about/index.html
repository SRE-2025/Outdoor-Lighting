Our story

Built on craftsmanship and follow-through

Excel Outdoor Lighting Co. is a family-owned company based in Buda, Texas. We design and install outdoor lighting across Hays County — and we back it with the kind of warranties and service you can only get from a local team that plans to be here for the long haul.

The business is family-owned and trusted locally since 2021, but the experience behind it runs much deeper — our team brings over 20 years of exterior-lighting know-how to every project. That shows up in the details: clean fixture placement, concealed wiring, warm color temperatures that flatter a home, and a system that's aimed and dialed in properly before we ever call a job done.

We're fully insured and bonded, and we treat every install like it's on our own street — because a lot of the time, it nearly is.

Our promise

We light every property like it's our own — and we stand behind the work long after the install is done.

That's why customers call us back season after season for holiday lighting, and refer their neighbors for permanent and landscape lighting.
