Designed for our climate

Lush doesn't have to mean thirsty

A water-wise yard isn't gravel and cactus. Done well, it's some of the most layered, alive-looking design there is — it just happens to survive a Hill Country August and stay comfortably inside Hays County watering restrictions.

We build around native and adapted plants that evolved for Central Texas soil and heat, group them by how much water they actually want, and prep the clay-and-caliche ground so water soaks in where roots can reach it. The result is a landscape that looks intentional, needs far less from you, and keeps its beauty when the next drought hits.

What water-wise design includes

  • Native & drought-tolerant plant selection
  • Hydrozoning — grouping plants by water need
  • Soil prep and generous mulch to hold moisture
  • Efficient drip irrigation and smart controllers
  • Xeriscape and lawn-reduction options
