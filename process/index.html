How we work

Design → Build → Maintain

One team and one point of contact, from the first sketch to the hundredth mow. Nothing gets lost in a handoff — because there isn't one.

Start With a Free Consultation
Home/Our Process
1

Consultation

We walk your property with you, listen to how you want to use the space, and read your soil, sun, drainage and budget. It's free and there's no pressure — you talk to the owner, not a call center.

2

Design & Estimate

We draw a plan built for your lot and the Central Texas climate, then give you a clear, itemized estimate. You'll know exactly what we're building and what it costs before anything starts.

3

Build

Our own crew installs it — plantings, sod, beds, irrigation, and hardscape like patios, pergolas and stonework — with the soil prep and craftsmanship that make it last. One crew, start to finish.

4

Maintain

Because we maintain what we build, we build for the long haul. Add a lawn-care or grounds plan and the same faces keep your landscape thriving season after season.

Why it matters

One crew means nothing falls through the cracks

When the people who design your landscape are also the ones who build and maintain it, you get accountability at every step — and a landscape that was planned from day one to stay beautiful for years.
