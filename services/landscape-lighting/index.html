Overview

Landscape Lighting

Good lighting doubles the hours you enjoy your landscape and adds real safety and curb appeal. We design and install low-voltage lighting that highlights trees, paths, and architecture — warm, efficient, and controlled on a timer or from your phone.

What's included

  • Path, step and safety lighting
  • Uplighting for trees and features
  • Patio and outdoor-living lighting
  • Efficient low-voltage LED systems
  • Timer and smart-control setup

Your yard, twice as long each day

In Central Texas, summer days are so hot that the yard is often only comfortable after the sun goes down, and that's exactly when most landscapes go dark and disappear. Good lighting flips that. It gives you back the cool evening hours on the patio, makes the whole property feel bigger after dark, and turns a landscape you only enjoyed in daylight into one that works around the clock.

Lighting is also the detail that quietly does the most for curb appeal. A well-lit home reads as cared-for and intentional from the street every single night.

Light for beauty, safety, and security

We design lighting to do three jobs at once. Uplighting on your live oaks, stonework, and the front of the house creates drama and depth. Path, step, and grade lighting keeps people safe on the uneven walks and slopes so common on Hill Country lots. And a well-lit property is simply less inviting to anyone who'd rather it be dark.

The art is in restraint. We aim light where it earns its place and keep the fixtures themselves discreet, so you see the effect, not a row of glaring bulbs. Layered, warm, and purposeful beats bright and scattered every time.

Low-voltage systems built to last outdoors

We install efficient low-voltage LED systems, which sip power, run cool, and last for years. Fixtures and wiring have to stand up to our sun, heat, and downpours, so we use quality components and bury and connect everything to hold up outdoors season after season, not just look good the week it's installed.

LED also means you can light the whole landscape for a fraction of the energy older systems used, and rarely think about changing a bulb.

Set it and forget it

We put your lighting on a timer or smart control so it comes on at dusk and off on schedule automatically, and you can adjust it from your phone if you want to. Set it up once and the yard lights itself every night without a thought.

The result is a landscape that keeps working after dark, doubling the hours you actually get to enjoy what you invested in.

Common questions

Landscape Lighting FAQs

Why choose low-voltage LED landscape lighting?

Low-voltage LED is safe, extremely efficient, and long-lasting, so you can light an entire landscape for a fraction of the energy older systems used and rarely change a bulb. It also runs cool and stands up well to our heat and storms when it's installed with quality components.

What parts of my yard are worth lighting?

The highest-impact spots are usually uplighting on mature trees and the front of the house, path and step lighting for safety on walks and slopes, and lighting for the patio and outdoor living areas you use after dark. We design the layout around beauty, safety, and security together rather than just flooding the yard.

Can the lights turn on and off automatically?

Yes. We set your system on a timer or smart control so it comes on at dusk and off on schedule with no effort from you, and you can adjust or override it from your phone. Set it up once and the landscape lights itself every night.

Will landscape lighting help with security?

It helps. A well-lit property removes the dark corners and shadows that make a home an easier target, and it does it while looking intentional rather than like a floodlit parking lot. Good design gets you both the ambiance and the deterrent at once.

How long does a lighting installation take?

Most residential lighting jobs go in within a day or a few, depending on the size of the property and how many fixtures and zones are involved. It's one of the less disruptive projects we do, and the payoff, a yard transformed after dark, shows up the very first night.
