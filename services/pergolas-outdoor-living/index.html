Shade is what makes a Texas backyard usable

You can build the finest patio in Hays County and never sit on it if it bakes in full sun from noon on. In our climate, shade is the difference between a backyard you look at and one you live in. A well-placed pergola or shade structure pulls the temperature down enough to actually enjoy the space through a Central Texas summer.

This is the high-craft work our long-time customers rave about. It's also where a landscape stops being a yard and becomes another room of the house.

Structures designed for your home and yard

A pergola shouldn't look like it came off a shelf. We design each structure to suit your house, its lines and materials, and the way the sun tracks across your lot, so the shade lands where you need it when you need it. Cedar, painted timber, steel, and stone columns all read differently, and we match the build to the architecture instead of dropping in a kit.

Because we design and build, the structure ties into the patio, plantings, and lighting as one coherent space rather than a collection of parts that don't quite talk to each other.

Complete outdoor living spaces

Beyond shade, this is where the extras come together: fire pits and fire features for the cooler months, built-in seating that saves you from hauling furniture, outdoor kitchens and grilling stations, and screening for privacy from the neighbors. We build the whole outdoor room so it works as a unit, from the surface underfoot to the shade overhead.

A fire feature stretches the season on both ends, and built-in seating means the space is always ready. These are the details that decide whether a backyard gets used every weekend or a few times a year.

Craftsmanship you'll notice for years

This is our signature work, and it's built to last decades in our weather, sized and anchored for our soil and our wind, finished to hold up under relentless sun. The craftsmanship is the part customers name in their reviews years later.

If you want a backyard you actually spend time in, this is where it starts, and it starts with a design drawn for your family by Gye himself.

Common questions

Pergolas & Outdoor Living FAQs

Will a pergola actually keep my patio cool enough to use?

A well-designed one makes a real difference. We position the structure and size the shade for how the sun tracks across your specific yard, so it blocks the worst afternoon heat when you'll actually be out there. For deeper shade we can design a solid or louvered roof rather than an open lattice.

What materials do you build pergolas from?

Cedar, painted timber, steel, and stone columns are all common, and each gives a different look and lifespan. We match the material to your home's architecture and your budget, and we build and anchor everything to hold up in our sun, soil, and Hill Country wind.

Can you add a fire pit or outdoor kitchen to the project?

Yes, and we often do. Fire features, built-in seating, grilling stations, and screening are exactly the elements that turn a patio into a true outdoor room. Because we design and build the whole space, these tie in cleanly instead of looking added on later.

Do I need a permit or HOA approval for an outdoor structure?

Often, yes. A roofed or attached structure can require a permit, and many Central Texas neighborhoods have HOA design review for anything in the backyard. We know the local process and help handle or flag the approvals before we build.

Is this something I can phase over time?

Absolutely. Many clients start with the patio and pergola, then add a fire feature, kitchen, or lighting in a later season. Because we design the full outdoor space up front, each phase fits the plan and nothing has to be undone to add the next piece.
