Overview

Tree Wrapping & Wreaths

Rooflines are just the start. Wrapped trunks and lit canopies, wreaths on the door, and garland along railings and columns are what make a property feel fully decorated for the season. We handle it all with professional-grade product and a clean, consistent look — installed, maintained, and taken down for you.

What's included

  • Trunk wrapping and canopy lighting
  • Wreaths, garland and accent displays
  • Coordinated look across the whole property
  • Commercial-grade lights and greenery
  • Full-season maintenance, takedown and storage

What takes a display from good to complete

A lit roofline is a great start, but it is the yard-level details that make a property feel fully dressed for the holidays. Wrapped tree trunks, lit canopies, a wreath on the door, and garland running along railings, columns, and porch posts fill in everything between the roof and the ground so the whole home reads as one finished display.

Central Texas gives us wonderful trees to work with. A big live oak with its trunk wrapped and lights carried up into the canopy becomes a glowing centerpiece that anchors the entire scene.

A coordinated look, not a pile of parts

The reason a professional display looks pulled-together is coordination. We plan the wreaths, garland, and tree work to match the roofline and each other — consistent bulb color, consistent greenery, consistent style — so nothing looks like it came from a different box or a different year.

We use commercial-grade lights and greenery that hold their color and shape through the season, which is what keeps the display looking crisp in week eight, not just on install day.

Installed, maintained, and taken down for you

Like the rest of our holiday work, tree wrapping and wreaths are fully hands-off for you. We install it, keep it looking right through the season, and take it all down and store it when the holidays are over.

It pairs naturally with roofline lighting for a complete look, books up early in the season, and is backed by the same quick, local service from our Buda shop.

Common questions

Tree Wrapping & Wreaths FAQs

Can you wrap large trees like mature live oaks?

Yes. Large live oaks are some of our favorite subjects — we wrap the trunk and carry lights up into the canopy so the tree becomes a glowing centerpiece. The amount of product depends on the tree's size, which we assess during the consultation.

Will the wreaths and garland match my roofline lights?

That is the whole idea. We coordinate the tree work, wreaths, and garland with your roofline lighting so the bulb color, greenery, and style all match, giving the property one pulled-together look rather than mismatched pieces.

Do you provide the wreaths and greenery?

Yes. We use commercial-grade lights and greenery that hold their color and shape through the whole season, so the display stays crisp from install day through the holidays. It is all included in your written estimate.

Is takedown included for the tree lights and wreaths?

It is. We install, maintain, and then remove and store everything after the holidays, tree wrapping and wreaths included. You never have to unwrap a trunk or find storage for it yourself.
