Install, Takedown & Storage

The reason people call us back every year is simple: we handle everything. We install your holiday lighting at the start of the season, keep it working through it, take it all down when it's over, and store it safely until next year — so your only job is to enjoy the display and let us do the ladders.

What's included

  • Professional seasonal installation

  • Safe off-season storage of your lighting
  • Priority rebooking year after year

The full-service cycle

This is the service that ties all of our holiday work together, and it is why people call us back season after season: we handle the entire lifecycle of your display. We install it at the start of the season, keep it working through the holidays, take every bit of it down when the season ends, and store it safely until next year.

You never haul boxes, never untangle a knot of last year's strands, never climb a ladder, and never lose garage space to bins of lights in the off-season. Your only job is to enjoy the display.

Maintained while it's up

A holiday display runs for weeks in real weather, and things occasionally go out. In-season maintenance is built in — if a section fails or a strand comes loose in the wind, we come out and make it right, so your home looks its best the whole season and not just the night we installed.

That reliability matters most during the busiest, coldest stretch of the year, exactly when you least want to be back up on a ladder yourself.

Stored right, ready to rebook

When the season ends, we do a clean, complete takedown and store your lighting properly in the off-season, which protects the product and keeps next year's setup fast and easy. Because we store and know your display, rebooking is simple and repeat clients get priority scheduling.

It is all handled by our local Buda crew, so every part of the cycle — install, service, takedown — stays quick and close to home. Our labor is backed by a 1-year warranty.

Common questions

Install, Takedown & Storage FAQs

What exactly does full-service include?

It covers the entire cycle: professional installation at the start of the season, in-season maintenance if anything goes out, complete takedown after the holidays, and safe off-season storage of your lighting. You enjoy the display and we handle every ladder and box.

Where are my lights stored in the off-season?

We store your lighting safely for you between seasons, which protects the product and makes next year's install faster. It also frees up the garage space those bins would otherwise take, and it means your setup is ready to go when you rebook.

What if something breaks while the lights are up?

In-season maintenance is part of the service. If a section stops working or a strand comes loose, we come out and fix it, so the display looks its best through the whole season rather than only on install day.

Do returning customers get priority?

Yes. Because we store and already know your display, rebooking is simple and repeat clients get priority scheduling. Reaching out early still helps, since the season fills up quickly.
