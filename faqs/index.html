Home/FAQs
What areas do you serve?

We're based in Buda and serve the greater Austin metro — from South Austin, Kyle and San Marcos up through Round Rock, Cedar Park, Leander, Hutto and Liberty Hill — across Hays, Travis and Williamson counties.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Every project starts with a free, no-pressure on-site consultation — and you'll talk directly with the owner, not a call center.

What type of grass grows best in Central Texas?

It depends on your yard. St. Augustine handles shade and looks lush but drinks more water; Bermuda thrives in full sun and shrugs off drought; Zoysia is a durable middle ground. We assess your sun, soil and watering habits and recommend the right turf for your lot.

Can you design a low-water or drought-tolerant landscape?

Absolutely — it's one of our specialties. We design native and xeriscape landscapes that stay beautiful on far less water and work comfortably within Hays County watering restrictions.

Do you build pergolas, patios and other hardscape?

Yes. Beyond planting and lawn care, we build pergolas, patio extensions, masonry, stone borders and full outdoor-living spaces — the high-craft work our long-time customers rave about.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — we're a fully licensed and insured, family-owned company operating in Central Texas since 1998, and we back our work with a workmanship warranty.

Do you offer any discounts?

We offer 10% off for military, veterans, first responders, nurses and teachers. Just mention it when you schedule (not combinable with other offers).

Do you also handle pest control?

Our sister company, Excel Pest & Lawn Control, handles pest control, termite letters for home closings, rodent exclusion and lawn-pest treatment — same family, same crews. Many customers use both.
